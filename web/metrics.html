How each measure is captured

Throughput
Boxes with a Dispatched event in the period ÷ days.
Lost / misplaced label rate
Misplaced, mislabelled, damaged, duplicate and unknown-label incidents ÷ boxes dispatched.
Investigation time
Incident open → close time (baseline: stopwatch study of operators searching).
Status retrieval time
Time to answer “where is box X and what stage is it in?” (baseline: stopwatch; pilot: lookup timing).
Stage dwell time
Time between consecutive status events for each box.
Quality / dispatch errors
Quality-error and dispatch-error incidents ÷ boxes dispatched.
Traceability coverage
Dispatched boxes with an unbroken history from production to dispatch.
Labour utilisation
(Labour hours − searching and status-lookup hours) ÷ labour hours.
Scan compliance
